# BUG-002: Add to Cart Success Notification Not Displayed

**Severity:** Medium

**Module:** Shopping Cart

**Environment:** Bug Build – Toolshop

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Open the Toolshop bug website.
2. Select an available product.
3. Open its product details page.
4. Click Add to Cart.
5. Observe the notification area.

### Expected Result
A success notification should display: "Product added to shopping cart."

### Actual Result
The expected success notification is not displayed after clicking Add to Cart.

### Evidence
`../screenshots/cart-missing-success-notification.png`

### Why It Matters
Without confirmation, customers may be unsure whether the product was successfully added to their cart.
